# Guitar sample packs (research data)

The four CC0 guitar sample packs the listening bench renders its rung-2 and held-out
material from. They were the old player's sample packs (`public/samples/`) until the synth
replaced it (roadmap/complete/core-campaign-synth.md); they live here now as bench data, not
playback. Each pack keeps its manifest, licence and source note. Provenance labels recorded
in earlier reports still read `public/samples/<pack>`: the files are the same bytes
(manifests carry their SHA-256).
