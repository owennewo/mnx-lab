# harness-v1 — draft, development only

The five examples and their pre-registered verdicts come from FIRST_STEP §§3–4,9.
Audio is absent at D; E will produce the WAVs from sine-v1 and pin their hashes.
The intended vendor score is copied verbatim, including its lack of event types and
tempo. Generated labels use positional note keys where that score has no note ids.

Durations follow the fixed recipe: p1 4 s, p2/c2 8 s, t1 5.5 s (final release 5 s,
then 0.5 s silence). c1 is explicitly 8.5 s. The phrase “as c1” in §9 applies the
same full-length-minus-150-ms formula to c2; it does not replace its recipe's duration.
Positive following labels end at the last release; the tail is explicitly unknown.
At t1 the wrong grid cells (.50, .55] through (4.95, 5.00] total 4.5 s.

Every record carries actual profile ranges: C5 outside the melodic baseline,
90 BPM as a tempo probe, and the silence/control deviations. Nothing is qualified.
