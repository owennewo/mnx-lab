### Question and alternatives

Does recreating 045's complete challenger workload and accumulating evidence reproduce
an attributable no-inference stall, and does moving that evidence out of the listener
process remove its demonstrated cause? Alternatives are collection driven by retained
harness evidence, listener-owned allocation/work, runqueue waiting or other off-CPU

of these. This expands 044's 99-example panel to every one of the 576 frozen examples,
with 045's challenger prefix workloads and offline alignment, including its retained
prior live/offline evidence. Later incumbent/baseline passes cannot have caused 045's
challenger stalls: they ran afterwards. They are not executed in this diagnosis.
