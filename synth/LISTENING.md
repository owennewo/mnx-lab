# Fixed standalone listening acceptance

In the built candidate, open **Session → Examples** and play each example in turn. An
example loads a piece together with the setup it was written for, so every listener
hears the same thing. Turn Loop off, keep the output level fixed, and Stop between
examples. Every example plays at the app's level (master 0 dB, no limiting), and each
is generated by `scripts/build_pieces.mjs` into `web/data/pieces/`.

| Order | Example | Listen for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Guitar – fingerpicked arpeggio | Soft nylon; the thumb's bass under lighter treble notes, natural decay |
| 2 | Guitar – strums down & up | Down strums from the low string, lighter up strums, a slow rake, a ringing final chord |
| 3 | Guitar – sustain & let ring | Stable pitch, natural unpumped tails, sympathetic ring |
| 4 | Guitar – bends, slides & vibrato | Bends reach pitch, slides glide, vibrato even |
| 5 | Guitar – hammer-ons, pull-offs & palm mutes | No new pick on legato notes; tight palm mutes; dead notes percussive |
| 6 | Guitar – soft to hard | Tone brightens and level rises smoothly with velocity |
| 7 | Guitar – recorded performance | The performed take's timing and touch |
| 8 | Ukulele – re-entrant strum | The high G string first in each down strum |
| 9 | Keys – pedal & rolled chords | Dry chords, pedalled chords, rolls up and down, a clean pedal release |
| 10 | Kit – groove, fill & hi-hat chokes | Ghost notes, the open hi-hat cut by the pedal, the fill into the crash |
| 11 | Band – four-bar groove | Distinct parts, coherent room |
| 12 | Band – slow ballad | The dotted echo on the guitar, soft pads, light ride |
| 13 | Guitar – tempo change: the echo follows | The echo's repeats tighten when the tempo rises |

The set is about three minutes of audio. Each example's checks (Inspect → Run checks)
measure activity, levels, diagnostics and selected pitch, timing and sustain behaviour;
they do not establish subjective tone or dropout-free physical playback.

Record verdicts against the candidate manifest SHA256, browser/device and output
route. For each example: acceptable / problem + timestamp and description.
Also try Stop mid-note, restart, a few normal live control edits and a design
change. If crackles are heard, note whether they occur during steady playback,
edits or switches. This is listening evidence, not a new performance campaign.

Acceptance is pending; no listening preference is inferred from automated checks.
