# Retire the tray's verbs and shell — one-surface campaign item 11b

> **Status: proposed and built 2026-08-31, in two slices.** Campaign:
> [workbench-campaign-one-surface.md](workbench-campaign-one-surface.md),
> item 11b — the closing item: the campaign's contract run against the shell
> itself, and the generalization thesis confirmed.

## Slice 1 — the gaps closed first

- **The `voice` construct word** at both bar rungs → `addVoiceMeasure` — the
  last bespoke construct verb became a declaration, closing the
  parent-constructs-child rule's final special case.
- **The blocked-tile class went extinct**: `arpeggio`'s blocker was stale (the
  adornment grammar already spoke it); `part-transposition`, `mute` and
  `section-colour` retired their tiles with the op-gaps carried to the
  closing entry below.

## Slice 2 — the demolition

The thesis held: every remaining verb tile was a rung-generic gesture's
costume, so nothing needed a new home —

- **Deleted whole**: `SelectionTray.ts`, `commandRegistry.ts` (the registry's
  one honest job — "what can I do here" — was already `keymapDocs`'
  single-sourced tables), the `command-registry` conformance suite, the
  page's tray/popover state machine, the uninhabited `PopoverKind` frame,
  and the smoke's tray act (with a pointer at the inspector smoke).
- **`PENDING_PRECEDENCE` lost `popover`** — the setup-popover class is
  extinct, so nothing can be pending in it; the pinned key-scope list was
  updated deliberately, which is what the pin is for.
- **`/` reverted to its pre-tray shell job: go-to.** The unclaimed fallback
  (type a few letters, land on a scenario) always was go-to, so the muscle
  memory outlives the tray that borrowed the key. Recorded as a revert, not
  a new binding — the freed-keys rule is intact.
- **The chip re-doored**: its click (which "was the `/` key") now opens the
  inspector — the rung indicator opens the rung surface.
- **Anchor machinery survived** (`overlayPlacement` gained the shared
  `OVERLAY_WIDTH`); the chip and inspector still hang off the selection
  anchor the tray introduced.
- **Credits re-homed**: `addVoiceMeasure` to the inspector,
  `applyCutPlan`/`applyPastePlan` to a `clipboard` surface entry (the keys
  are the surface; the mount resolves the plans).
- The design-token joins and the range-semantics tests were re-fixtured or
  retired with their subjects, the same three-way sort 11a used.

## Op-gaps carried forward (not tray debts — op debts)

- **`part-transposition`** — no op; needs its own proposal if wanted.
- **`mute`** — audio's player question is unchanged
  (core-viewer-embedded-app.md records why there is no player element).
- **`section-colour`** — decide document property vs viewer preference before
  giving it a verb.

## What the campaign leaves behind

One editing surface. Every former popover grammar speaks through the
inspector's words and pills; every verb is a rung-generic gesture in the
keymap (`I`/`Shift+I` sideways, declarations downward, `Del`'s ladder for
removal, closures and history on their keys); discoverability is the `?`
legend and the palette's `>` list; `keymapDocs` is the one command index.
The triage ledger retired to `superseded/` with this item.
